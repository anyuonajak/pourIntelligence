Pour Intelligence

Terms of use

Advisory tool, not professional engineering

Pour Intelligence estimates pour-readiness for concrete slabs (ACI 305R / 306R) and masonry lay-up (TMS 602 / ACI 530.1) from public weather forecasts. Outputs are informational. They are not a substitute for project specifications, means and methods, or the engineer of record.

No guarantee of outcomes

Weather forecasts change. Mixes, curing, and jobsite practice vary. We do not warrant that a GO, WARNING, or NO_GO result will match what happens in the field. Do not rely on this service as the sole basis for a placement decision.

Data we store

When you run a check we may store the request (location, pour time, mix inputs), the model output, and — if you submit it — an outcome (success, cracked, delayed, or other). We use that to validate and improve the model. Do not submit personal information in notes.

API use

The public demo is rate-limited. Vendor access uses an API key you must keep secret. Do not scrape, overload, or use the service to provide a competing weather-physics score as if it were your own without an agreement.
